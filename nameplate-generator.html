🏷️ Name Plate Generator

A single-file, in-browser parametric generator for 3D-printable name plates, keychains, and pet tags. Built with three.js — no install, no build step, everything runs client-side.

  • Multi-line text with independent per-line sizing and line spacing
  • A wide range of fonts: sans-serif, script/handwriting, bubble/bold
  • Rounded, Rectangle, Oval, or Contour (outline hugs the letters) base plates
  • Mounting holes or a keychain loop, beveled edges, top/bottom border text
  • One-click style presets to get started fast
  • STL export: one combined file, or body + text as two files for multi-color (AMS/MMU) printing

Open source, MIT licensed.
